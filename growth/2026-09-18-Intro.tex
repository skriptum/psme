\subsection{Maths Refresher}\label{maths-refresher}

Quick tips

\[
\ln a*b  = \ln a + \ln b \\
(\ln x)'  = \frac{1}{x} \\
\frac{d}{dt}\ln(g(t))  = [\ln g(t)] \cdot g(t)' = \frac{g(t)'}{g(t)}
\]

And a more complex one

\[
\frac{d}{dt} \big( \frac{x(t)}{y(t)} \big)  = \frac{x'(t) \cdot y(t) - x(t) \cdot y'(t)}{[y(t)]^2} \\
 = \frac{x'}{y} \cdot \frac{y}{y} \cdot \underbrace{\frac{x}{x}}_{new} 
- \frac{x(t)}{y(t)} \frac{y'}{y}\\
 = \underbrace{\frac{x'}{x}}_{g_x} \cdot \frac{x}{y} - - \frac{x}{y} \underbrace{\frac{y'}{y}}_{g_y}\\
 = \frac{x(t)}{y(t)} [g_x(t) - g_y(t)]
\]

And another one

\[
\frac{x'}{x}  = a \\
[\ln x(t)]  = a  \text{ | from (4)}  \\
\int_{dt} \ln x(t)  = \int_{dt} a \\
\ln x  = at + c \\
e^{\ln x(t)}  = e^{(at+c)} = e^{at} \cdot e^{c} \\
x(t)  = e^{at} \cdot \bar{c}_{constant}
\]

Useful: Assume that there is \(t_0\) so that \(x(t_0)\) is known (e.g
GDP in 1990)

\[
x(t_0) = \bar{c}\cdot e^{at_0} \\
\bar{c} = x(t_0) \cdot e^{-at_0}
\]

Substitute

\[
x(t) = \underbrace{[x(t_0) \cdot e^{-at_0}]}_{\bar{c}} \cdot e^{at} \\
\underline{x(t) = x(t_0) \cdot e^{a(t-t_0)}}
\]

\max_{K,L} p F(K,L) - rK - wL
\]

\item
  r = interest rate
\item
  w = wage
\end{itemize}
