Tanks or Teachers¶

Extension Solow Model

  • idea: why do countries spend on military

    • current NATO Expansion

  • there is a opportunity cost for it

    • swords to ploughshares

    • guns and butter

    • today: Tanks or Teachers

model the decision making process

  • between military spending

  • and human capital

=> military spending = protection

  • protects the physical capital stock (Destruction)

  • and human capital (loss of lifes)

Simple Model

  • following MRW

  • with fixed tax amount, either going to military spending or education

    • empirical: rising military atm lower education spending

  • function modelling the risk of war

\[\begin{split} D = (n+d) + z(m) \\ z(m) =z_0e^{-\sigma m} \end{split}\]

  • n+d = normal depreciation

  • \(z(m) = z_0 e^{-km}\) = expected war loss

    • chance of war

    • and destruction in case of war

    • Z0 = chance of war without military

    • \(e^{-\sigma m}\) = decreasing war loss due to military

      • deterrence effect

      • or better protection (e.g air defence)

    • sigma = factor defining it. in simple case: k=1

  • D = Deprecetion / Damage Factor

Laws of Motion $\( \dot{k} = s(1-t)y - (n+d)k - z(m)k \\= s(1-t)y - D(m) \cdot k \\ \dot{h} = (t-m)y - D(m) \cdot h \\ \)$

  • both stocsk are impacted by deprecation + destruction

  • factor into equation as in normal version

Steady State $\( \dot{k} = 0 \implies k^* = \frac{s(1-t)}{D(m)}y \\ h^* = \frac{(t-m)}{D(m)}y \\ \)\( Subst in \)y = k^{\alpha} h^\beta\( \)\( y = [\frac{s(1-t)}{D(m)}y]^\alpha [\frac{(t-m)}{D(m)}y]^\beta \\ = y^{\alpha+\beta}[s(1-t)]^\alpha [t-m]^\beta D(m)^{-(\alpha+\beta)} \\ y^* = \{[s(1-t)]^\alpha [t-m]^\beta D(m)^{-(\alpha+\beta)} \}^{1/1-\alpha-\beta} \)$

  • investment into physical capital

  • investment into human capital

  • damage term, applying to both of them

Maximize:

  • we want to maximize y over military consumption

    • bracket factor doesnt matter (no m)

    • physical capital factor doesnt matter

Derivative of \(D: D' = - \sigma z_0\)

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\begin{split} = \overbrace{[t-m]^\beta}^f \overbrace{D(m)^{-(a+b)}}^g\\\end{split}\\\begin{split}\overbrace{-\beta[t-m]^{\beta-1}}^{f'} \cdot \overbrace{D(m)^{-(a+b)}}^{g} + \overbrace{[t-m]^{\beta}}^{f} \cdot \overbrace{-(\alpha+\beta) D(m)^{-(a+b)-1}D'}^{g'} \\ \end{split}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

Factor out positive things $\( [-\beta D - (\alpha+\beta)(t-m) D'] \cdot (t-m)^{\beta-1} D(m)^{-(a+b)-1} \)$

  • on both sides similar terms

  • both negative exponents, aka both negative, combined both positive

  • inside the bracket, the relevant things are left

Extract and insert: \(D = n+d+z(m)\), \(D' = -\sigma z(m)\) $\( N(m) = [-\beta D - (\alpha+\beta)(t-m) D'] \\ = - \beta[n+d+z(m)] -(\alpha+\beta)(t-m) [-\sigma z(m)] \)\( Optimum: N(m) = 0 \)\( (\alpha+\beta)(t-m) \sigma z(m) = \beta[n+d+z(m)] \\ \frac{(\alpha+\beta)\sigma z(m)}{n+d+z(m)} = \frac{\beta}{(t-m)} \)$ Intuitive explanation

  • right = cost of tanks

    • dependent on improtance of human capital

    • and share of taxes spent on military

  • left = benefit of tanks

    • Denom: higher m = lower z = lower destruction

    • Nom: higher chance of war = higher benefit

      • (a+b) = both stocks combined, protection

military spending: \(m^* > 0\) IF N(m) > 0

  • aka MB > MC of tanks

rearrange $\( N(0) = -\beta[n+d+z(0)]-(\alpha+\beta)t[-\sigma z(0)] \\ = z_0 [-\beta + (\alpha+\beta)t \sigma ] - \beta(n+d) > 0 \\ z_0 > \frac{-\beta(n+d)}{(\alpha+\beta)\sigma t-\beta} \)$ = Critical Value for Military

Numerical Solving

  • \(\alpha = \beta = 1/3\)

  • (n+d) = 0.05

  • sigma = 1

  • t = total taxes

first of all: RHS only positive if $\( (\alpha+\beta) \sigma t - \beta > 0 \\ (\alpha+\beta) \sigma t > \beta \\ \implies t > 0.5 \)$ Only if tax rate in economy is > 50%, military spending is worth it

  • Otherwise: not really
