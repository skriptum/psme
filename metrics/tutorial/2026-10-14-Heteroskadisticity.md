# 14.10.2026 Heteroskedasticity



What is heteroskedasticity?

- Variance of errors is **not** constant
- e.g Variance of unobservables = different across segments of population



**Problems** 

- Estimators are biased
- Statistitcs for hypothesis not valid
- BUT: coeffiencts still unbiased! 



**Detection**

- Informal: Plots, etc
- Formal: Breusch-Pagan, White test



**Correction**

- Robust Standard errors
- FLGS
