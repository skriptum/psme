







# OLS

Characteristics of Estimators:

- **unbiased**: $E(\hat{b}_1) = b_1$
  - expected value = true parameters
- **consistency**: $Pr(\hat{b}_1 \neq b_1)=0$
  - variance goes to 0 if sample size to infinity
- **efficient**: lowest variance



Concepts

- **Central limit theorem**: residuals and estimators = normal distributed 
- **R-squared:** percentage of variability that explains the model
- **F-statistic:** better than using a constant at explaining variation? 



### Alternatives to R2

Akaike Information Criteria (AIC)
$$
AIC = ln(\frac{SSR}{n}) + (k+1) \frac{2}{n}
$$
Bayes Information Criteria (BIC)
$$
AIC = ln(\frac{SSR}{n}) + (k+1) \frac{\ln n}{n}
$$
= punishes larger models harder

Code: `estat ic`
