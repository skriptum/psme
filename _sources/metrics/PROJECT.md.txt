# Metrics Project: IMF and X

"Who Pays for adjustment? Effects of IMF Bailouts on Income Inequality"

Idea: Does getting an IMF Bailout effect the income distribution in a country?



Mechanism: Conditionality of IMF Programs

- adjustment of tax code, labor code
  - reduces power of unions (wage-compressing role)
- fiscal-policy adjustment
- trade liberialization



Problems:

- Endogeneity: A country only comes to the IMF in case of crisis
- Wage Inequality beforehand may impact economic growth (and crisis)



Identification: countries that take on

- SBA = Stand-by Arrangements (1-2 years)
- EFF = extended fund facility (3-4 years)



Outcome Variable:

- Income Shares (50%, 40%, 10%): 
  - instead of Gini, but also check robustness
- Labor Share of Income



Additional Explorations:

- Changes in Effects over recent decades?
- Regional Differences?



Data: 

- World Inequality Database (income shares of groups + Gini)
