# Defense Requirements

**About: Presentations by groups will be 10 minutes, followed by 10 minutes of questions from the**
**TD instructors.**

- Presentation includes at least one slide per section of the paper. (2 points)
- Each group member explains at least one section of the paper. (4 points)
- Each group member answers at least one question during the Q&A, as assigned by the TD
  instructors. (4 points)
- The presentation shows and discusses the identification strategy of the paper and
  discusses its potential weaknesses. (5 points)
- The presentation shows at least one data visualization motivating the research question.
  (2.5 points)
- The presentation shows at least one table of results in the slides, interprets and
  discusses the implications of results. (2.5 points)
