

## Introduction and Literature Review (10%)

- **Motivation and Research Question (8 points):** Clearly states the research question, its importance, and the scope of the paper. Contextualizes the topic within the field or subfield of economics being studied.
- **Engagement with Literature (8 points):** Identifies and briefly explains the findings of three papers relating to the research question. Cites the papers and explains what the paper adds to this literature.
- **Coherence and Structure (4 points):** Provides a logical flow connecting the motivation, literature review, and objectives of the paper. Avoids excessive detail or tangents.

## Data Description (5%)

- **Data Description (20 points):** Clearly describes the dataset(s) used, including their sources, key variables, sample size, and limitations. Provides summary statistics to characterize the data. If applicable, justifies any transformations or exclusions.

## Econometric Methodology (30%)

- **Explanation of Empirical Strategy (10 points):** Selects an appropriate econometric model or technique for the research question. Explains the methodology concisely, including its assumptions and limitations. Uses correct notation and links the empirical strategy to the research question.
  - **Note:** A clear presentation includes showing the steps taken to arrive at the final model specification, presenting the equations (regression models), and discussing the variables included.
- **Defense/Discussion of Empirical Strategy (10 points):** Discusses threats to exogeneity in the chosen approach. (Reminder: 99.9999% of models can be questioned; demonstrating awareness of and attempts to resolve weaknesses in the model is key.) Uses statistical tests and/or data visualization to demonstrate an understanding of the empirical strategy.

## Interpretation and Discussion of Results (20%)

- **Clarity in Presentation (4 points):** Presents results using well-labeled tables and graphs. Ensures that all tables and figures are referenced in the text and include clear notes where necessary.
- **Depth of Analysis (8 points):** Interprets the results accurately and rigorously. Compares the econometric and economic meaning of different models used. Discusses both the statistical and economic significance of the findings. Explores potential mechanisms of the identified effect and heterogeneity of effects (i.e., across different groups).
- **Engagement with Robustness (6 points):** Includes robustness checks or alternative specifications where appropriate. Alternative specifications may involve different variables or measurements to demonstrate invariance to the measurement approach, or different models and empirical strategies. Explains why these checks are relevant and interprets their outcomes and implications for the strength of the findings.
- **Conclusion and Wrap-Up (2 points):** Provides a concise recap of the main results and their implications. Relates the conclusion to the research question posed in the introduction. Acknowledges the study's limitations and identifies avenues for future research.
