>
> 1. monetary autonomy

Ex: 1+3 (Europe); 1+2 (China), (2+3) Bosnia



## Modeling

Assumption: no capital controls

- $i > i^*$: capital inflows
- $i^* > i$: capital outflows

=> International Financial Market = horizontal line on $(Y,i)$ plane

Long-Term Parity: 
$$
(1+i_x)  = \frac{F_{x/y}}{S_{x/y}}(1+i_y)
$$

- $i_x; i_y$ = interest rate in country x ; y
- $F_{x/y}$ = Forward exchange rate (in a year)
- $S_{x/y}$ = Spot exchange rate 

> LHS: I invest in domestic markets
>
> RHS: I invest in foreign markets today and convert back in 1 year from now.



CB Policy

- CB can conduct independent MP: lower rates
- more demand from domestic actors: IS => right
- but exchange rate = market based: appreciation of currency
- lower foreign demand due to higher cost: IS => left

=> no Fiscal Policy is possible anymore! (CB will always counteract)



## Limitations

- Perfect Capital Mobility Assumption
- short-term focus
- very simple financial markets
- supply side missing
