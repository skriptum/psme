## Aggregate Demand

Note: Long run AD = flat between output / inflation

Medium Run

- Fixed Exchange Rate: $\mu = \pi^* + g$ (fixed to peg inflation)

- Floating Exchange rate: $u = \bar{\pi} + g$

AD: Combinations of Price Level and Output: in equilibrium

=> downward sloping: lower prices -> higher demand



## Aggregate Supply

= Production Function + Labor Market

- Long Run: fixed point depends on L,Y
- Short run: Labor supply adjusts, depending on nominal wage
- medium run: prices will adjust faster than wages => real wage adjustments

AS: Combinations of unemployment rate and Output (Philipps Curve)

=> upward sloping





## Philipps Curve

= negative relation of **inflation + unemployment rate**

"Trade off between both"
