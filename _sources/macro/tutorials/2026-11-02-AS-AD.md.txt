# 02.11.2026 AS-AD

Numerical Example: derive IS, TR, AD

- DD = C+I+G
  -  $100+0.2(Y-200)+200-1000i$
- IS = Equilbrium of DD and Y
- Y = DD 
  - $Y = 637.5-1250i$
  - $i = 0.51 - 0.0008Y$
- TR: $i = \bar{i}+ a(i-\bar{i}) + b(\frac{Y-\bar{Y}}{Y})$
  - $= 2+1.5(\pi-2)+0.5(\frac{Y-10000}{10000})$
  - $= 2-3-0.5*(1) + 1.5 \pi + \frac{0.5}{10000}Y$
  - $= -1.5+1.5\pi+\frac{0.5}{10000}Y$
- AD: IS = TR
  - $-1.5+1.5\pi+\frac{0.5}{10000}Y = i = 0.51 - 0.0008Y$
  - $\pi =1.34-0.0056Y$
- AS Curve: 
  - PC: $\bar{\pi} -10(U-\bar{U})+s$
  - U = $U-\bar{U} = -0.5(\frac{Y-\bar{Y}}{Y})$
  - $2-10(-0.5(\frac{Y-10000}{10000}))$
- Equilibirum: AS = AD
  - $1.34-0.0056Y = 2+ \frac{5Y}{10000}-5$
  - $Y = 3150.94$
- Plug Y into either AS / AD
  - AS: $\pi =1.34-0.0056 * 3150.94$
  - $\pi =3,142-0.42$
