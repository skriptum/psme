

# 28.09.2026 Financial Debt 


$$
B_t = (1+r) B_{t-1}-D_t \\
\frac{B_t}{y_t} = (1+r) \frac{B_t-1}{y_t} * \overbrace{\frac{y_{t-1}}{y_t-1}}^{=1}
-\frac{D_t}{y_t}   \\
$$

with $\frac{y_{t}}{y_t-1} = (1+g)$

$$
b_t = \frac{(1+r)}{(1+g)} b_{t-1}-d_t
$$

Dynamics

$$
\Delta b_t = b_t - b_{t-1} = \frac{(1+r)}{(1+g)} b_{t-1}-d_t - b_{t-1} \\

\underline{\Delta b_t = (r-g) b_{t-1} - d_t}
$$


Concepts:

> **Fiscal Dominance:** Fiscal Policy dominates Spending, Monetary Policy will follow and CB will help with debt

> **Monetary Dominance:** Fiscal Policy Makers know that CB will react to higher debt



Example:

- Debt = 120%
- R = 4%
- G = 1%
- Primary Deficit = 2%

$$
(0.04-0.01)1.2-0.02 = 0.016
$$

Note: 0.016 is absolute increase in debt as % of GDP (Not multiplicator!)
