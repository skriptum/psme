# Solow Extension



Idea:

- include a military sector (stylised similar to a Government sector)
  - wasteful spending 
  - investment spending
- as a % of GDP (NATO target etc)
- and a protection of capital stock with chance x of war



Process:

- Develop Model: which risk of war makes it optimal?








$$
\dot{k} = s\overbrace{(1-m)}^{\text{taxes}} k^a + \overbrace{\phi mk^a}^{\text{mil. inv.}} - (n+g+d)k - \overbrace{(\lambda (m) \cdot \mu)k}^{\text{war damage}}
$$

- m = military share of GDP
- $\lambda(m)$ = probability of war, depends on military share
- $\mu$ = fraction of capital destroyed in a war
- $\phi$ = share of Military budget into Producitve investments (dual use, infrastructure)
