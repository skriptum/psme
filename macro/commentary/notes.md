## Reality

250 words

As mentioned: multiple assumptions in model, amon them2 that are violated in china:

-  First: perfect capital mobility and interest rate parity = for horizontal IFM line
  - China: capital controls hinder free movement out of country
  - managed exchange rate: appreciation of currency does not take place
    - as suggested in the article by some countries: competetive devaluation

- => IFM Line is not as strict as suggested in the model



Second: TR Rule

  - bechmark lending rate LPR = unchanged for more than 17 months (@ChinaSetLeave2026)
