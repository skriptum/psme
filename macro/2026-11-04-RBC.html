11.11.2026 Real Business Cycle¶

Bob Lucas in 70s:

  • Rational Critique of Keynesian Models

  • after „failure“ of 70s style

=> how we ended up with the mess of neoclassical RBC :/

REH: Rational Expectations Hypothesis

Basic RBC¶

  • Consumers:

    • representative agent

    • preferences (by utility function)

  • Firms: production technology

  • Markets: Good, Labour, Capital

  • Uncertainty

in a Walrasian Model:

  • No taxes, externalities, imperfections, frictions

  • perfect competition

  • = unrealistic shit

Consumer¶

  • continuum of [0,1] identical consumers

  • infinitely lived

  • Assets: capital

  • unvertainty about future (t>0)

    • but REH: consumers understand stochastic process guiding future

    • are on average correct

  • Discount factor \(\beta \in (0,1)\)

    • future assets = worth less

Consumer Utility: 2dimensional¶

  • Intratemporal: in time t

    • consumption \(c_t\)

    • or leisure: \(1-n_t\) (normalized to 1 = fraction)

    • MRS: \(u(c_t, 1-n_t)\)

  • Intertemporal: today vs tmrw

    • consumption in each period: \(c_0, c_1,...\)

    • defined by savings

=> Lifetime expected utility with discount and expectation operator E

\[ E_0 \sum_t^\infty \beta^t u(c_t, 1-n_t) \]

Flow Budget Constraint¶

\[ c_t + k_{t+1} = w_t n_t + r_t k_t +(1-\delta)k_t+ \Pi_t \]

Expenditure (Consumption and savings):

  • Laobur Income \(w_t n_t\)

  • Capital Income \(r_t k_t\)

  • depreciated capital stock \((1-\delta)k_t\)

  • Firms Profits (lump sum to owners) \(\Pi_t\)

Maximization¶

Consumers

  • maximize Utility function

  • subject to sequence of Flow Budget Constraints

  • options: consumption c, leisure n, saving k

Lagrangian $\( L = E_0 \sum \beta^t \bigg[ u(c_t,1-n_t)- \lambda_t (c_t + k_{t+1} - w_t n_t -...) \bigg] \)$ FOC in period t

  1. \(\delta L / \delta c_t: u'_c() - \lambda_t = 0\)

  2. \(\delta L / \delta n_t: - u'_n() + \lambda_t w_t = 0\)

  3. \(\delta L / \delta k_{t+1}: - \lambda_t + \beta E_t [\lambda_{t+1} (r_{t+1}+1-\delta)] = 0\)

Solution

  • 1 and 2: consumption-leisure optimality condition:

    • \(u'_c() / u'_n() = w_t\) [after solving for lambda]

    • wage defines decision between consumption and leisure

    • substitution and income effect

  • 1 and 3) consumption saving optimality

    • \(u'_c() = \beta E [u'_c (_{t+1})(r_{t+1}+1-\delta)]\) [solving for lambda]

under assumption of no uncertainty E=1 $\( \frac{u'_c()}{\beta u_c(_{n+1})} = 1+r_{t+1}+1-\delta \)$

  • intertemporal MRS = intertemproal price

Firms¶

  • Continuum of [0,1] firms

  • static entities

    • rent factors of production \(n_t, k_t\) at given prices

    • produce goods

    • sell output

  • infinitely lived

Aggregate Production Function: \(y_t = z_t f(k_t,n_t)\)

Profit Function (to maximize): \(\Pi_t = z_t f()- w_t n_t - r_t k_t\)

FOC:

  1. \(\delta \Pi / \delta n = z_t f'_n() - w_t = 0\)

  2. \(\delta \Pi / \delta k = z_t f'_k() - r_t = 0\)

  • downward sloping labour / capital demand

Equilibrium¶

Goods market

  • Supply = Demand

  • \(z_tf(k_t, n_t) = c_t+k_{t+1}-(1-\delta)k_t\)

Labor Market: \(n_t^s = n_t^d\)

Capital Market: \(k_t^s = k_t^d\)

Dynamic Equilibrium: \(c_t, n_t, k_{t+1}, w_t, r_t\)

  • Transversality condition

    • endpoint needed to solve (next to \(k_0\))

    • Value of all initial assets =

      • PDV of all future dissavings

      • PDF of assets at end of planning horizons

    • = zero

Discussion¶

RBC = classical view of economic cycles

  • random fluctuations in rate of technological change

  • individuals rationally alter their decisions

Business Cycle = natural and efficient response of economy

=> no need for macroecnomic governance

Weakness:

  • Technology = primary source of variation

  • leisure to explain changes in employment (what about forced unemployment)

  • trivializes the cost of fluctuations
