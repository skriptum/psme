# 23.09.2026 Monetary and Fiscal Policies



## Monetary policy

> **central bank:** public agency with 
>
> - exclusive mandate to control money and credit conditions
> - "lender of last resort"
>
> use *monetary policy* to manage economy



Tool: adjust the **creation of money** , which is:

- means of payment
- unit of account
- store of value
- deferred payment (debt)

Types: 

- Commodity Money
- Fiat Money

Monetary Aggregates

```
M0 = cash + bank reserves
M1 = M0 + overnight deposits
M2 = M1 + term deposits < 2 years
M3 = M2 + Repo, MMF-shares, Debt securities < 2 years
```



## Money Creation

by private banks (Fiat Money)

- creation bounded by MRR (marginal reserve rate)
- to create liquidity provision

$$
S = \frac{a_1}{1-r}
$$

Banks:

- reserve payments and balance at end of day
- buy / sell extra reserves via interbank market
- other option: buy from CB with short-term-repos



Central Bank Instruments

- Money Market Interest Rates
- quantity of reserves
- legal reserve requirement
- collateral framework rules



ECB rates:

- Deposit facility (Banks deposits )
- main refinancing operations (weekly borrowing)
- marginal lending facility (shrt term borrowing by ECB)

Deposit > MRO > MLF



## Fiscal Policy

Macroeconomic Stabilisation

- Consumption & tax smoothing
- Output and employment stabilisation
- Transfers etc.

Automatic + countercyclical



- **Fiscal Balance** = income - expenditure
- **Fiscal surplus / deficit** = Revenes > expenditures (or vice versa)
- **Primary Balance** = Fiscal balance - Interest Payments



**Debt Dynamics**

no growth, no inflation:

$$
\overbrace{\Delta B}^{\text{change in debt}} = 
\overbrace{G - T}^{\text{primary deficit}} + \overbrace{rB}^{\text{debt service}}
$$

with growth (after some fancy maths):

$$
\Delta (\frac{B}{Y}) = 
\overbrace{\frac{G-T}{Y}}^{\text{primary deficit}} + \overbrace{(r-g) \frac{B}{Y}}^{\text{debt cap}}
$$

- Rule for stable deficit: $\frac{G-T}{Y}=(r-g) \frac{B}{Y}$



with inflation and growth:
$$
\Delta (\frac{B}{Y}) = \frac{G-T}{Y} + (r-g) \frac{B}{Y} - \frac{\Delta M0 / P}{Y}
$$

- $\delta M0 / P$ = new money issuance as rate of total issued currency



if we use $\pi = \mu - g$ (inflation = money growth - growth), 

$$
\frac{T-G}{Y} = (r-g) \frac{B}{Y}  - (\pi+g)\frac{M0}{PY}
$$
