ln(Def_t) = ln(y_t^N) - ln(y_t^R) \\
\frac{\delta}{\delta t} \implies \frac{\dot{D}}{D} = \frac{\dot{y}^N_t}{y^N_t} - \frac{\dot{y}^R_t}{y^R_t}
